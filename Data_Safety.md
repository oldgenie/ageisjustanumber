# Data Safety

**App:** Age Is Just A Number  
**Package:** com.ageisjustanumber.app

## Data collection

Age Is Just A Number does not collect personal or sensitive user data.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, telephone number, location, photograph, or other personal information.

The birthday entered by the user is used locally on the device to calculate chronological age and generate the app's fictional “Feel Age” forecast. It is not transmitted to the developer or to a remote server.

## Data sharing

The app does not share user data with third parties.

No personal information is sold, transferred, or provided to advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

## Data processing

The birthday and calculated age information are processed locally on the user's device for the functionality of the app.

The app does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or personalised marketing.

## Account information

The app does not require or support user accounts.

No account registration, authentication, or login information is collected.

## Location

The app does not collect, request, or share the user's precise or approximate location.

## Financial information

The app does not collect financial or payment information.

## Health information

The app does not collect health or medical information. The “Feel Age” and forecast features are entertainment features and are not intended to provide medical, health, or fitness advice.

## Contacts and personal communications

The app does not access the user's contacts, messages, email, call history, or other personal communications.

## Photos, videos and files

The app does not require access to the user's photographs, videos, files, or media library.

## Advertising

The app does not contain advertising and does not use advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

## Analytics

The app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

## Security

User-entered birthday information used by the application remains on the device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers.

## Data deletion

Because the app does not transmit or maintain personal user data on developer-controlled servers, there is no server-side personal-data account or dataset requiring deletion.

Users may remove locally stored application data through the normal Android application controls or uninstall the application.

## Privacy policy

The app's privacy policy provides further information about how user information is handled and can be accessed through the privacy-policy URL associated with the Google Play listing.

## Important qualification

This document describes the intended data practices of the current release.

If a future version adds analytics, advertising, cloud storage, crash-reporting services, accounts, external APIs that transmit user information, or any other data-processing functionality, the Google Play Data Safety declaration and privacy policy will be reviewed and updated before that functionality is released.
